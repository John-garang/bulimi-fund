Partner With Us

Cocoa supply is shaped years before the first commercial harvest. Early partnership can improve planting material, farmer practices, traceability and buyer alignment while new gardens are being established.

Partnership Pathways

How you can partner

Exporters, Processors & Chocolate Companies

Market specifications, offtake pathways, quality systems and responsible-sourcing support.

Corporate & Philanthropic Funders

Patient capital for farmer onboarding, nurseries, training, extension and measurement.

Input & Nursery Partners

Quality planting material, organic soil nutrition, pest management and last-mile logistics.

Government & Research Partners

Technical guidance, district coordination, agronomy, soil and climate learning.

Finance & Insurance Partners

Appropriate services for smallholder farmers during establishment and production.

Technology Partners

Farmer records, plot mapping, traceability, payments and field-team tools.

What We Bring

What Obulimi brings to a partnership

A defined farmer journey from registration to market.

Parish-level organization and local implementation.

Obulimi-managed nursery production and coordinated establishment.

Continuing field support and documented farm progress.

A market-access model based on buying and aggregating quality dried beans.

A commitment to traceability, safeguards, honest measurement and learning.
