Privacy Notice

Last updated: July 2026

Who we are

Obulimi is a Ugandan social enterprise. For questions about this privacy notice or how we handle your information, contact us at [data contact email] or [office address].

What information we collect

We may collect information from website visitors (analytics, contact forms), farmers (registration details, plot information, farm records), donors (contact and payment information) and partners (contact and organizational details). We collect only what is necessary for the stated purpose.

Why we use it

Information is used to process farmer registrations and programme participation, respond to enquiries, process donations, manage partnerships, improve our website and comply with legal obligations. We will not use your information for purposes incompatible with those stated at collection.

Who may receive it

We may share information with service providers who support our operations (such as payment processors and technology providers) under appropriate agreements. We do not sell personal information. We may share information where required by law.

Retention and protection

We retain records for as long as necessary for the purpose collected and as required by applicable law. We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information from unauthorized access, loss or misuse.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information where applicable. Contact us at [data contact email] to make a request. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Cookies and analytics

This website may use cookies and analytics tools to understand how visitors use the site. Analytics data is aggregated and does not identify individuals. You can manage cookies through your browser settings.

This notice will be updated as our operations develop. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised. Continued use of this website after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised notice.

Obulimi is building farmer capability, productive cocoa gardens and dependable market access in Uganda.
