Farmer Services

A practical guide for prospective and participating farmers — what Bulimi offers and how to join.

Eligibility

Who can register interest

A farmer in a current Bulimi operating area.

Able and willing to dedicate a minimum of one acre to cocoa.

Has a clear and peaceful right to use the proposed land.

Willing to attend training and follow responsible production guidance.

Committed to maintaining the garden through the establishment years.

Willing to participate in a Bulimi farmer group and basic farm records.

The Farmer Service Package

Registration & Plot Assessment

Basic farmer information, plot mapping and suitability checks.

Cocoa Training

From land preparation and planting to farm care and post-harvest quality.

High-Quality Seedlings

Planting material produced through Bulimi-managed nursery beds.

Garden-Establishment Guidance

On-farm direction before, during and after planting.

Extension

Scheduled field support and problem solving as the crop develops.

Last-Mile Inputs

Organic fertilizers and organic pesticides at subsidised prices where available.

Market Access

A structured opportunity to sell quality dried cocoa beans through Bulimi.

The Process

How joining works

1

Submit Interest

Complete the registration interest form below or contact Bulimi directly.

2

Community Orientation

Attend a community information session to learn about the programme and ask questions.

3

Eligibility & Plot Checks

Bulimi assesses your eligibility and visits the proposed plot for suitability mapping.

4

Registration

Eligible farmers are formally registered and assigned to a parish-level farmer group.

5

Training & Land Preparation

Attend cocoa training and prepare your plot according to Bulimi's guidance.

6

Seedling Allocation & Planting

Receive your seedlings and plant with on-farm support from the Bulimi field team.

Register Now

Register your interest

Complete this form and the Bulimi team will be in touch. Submitting this form does not guarantee enrolment — eligibility and plot checks are required.

Note on seedling allocation

Registration expresses and records interest. Seedling allocation depends on eligibility, plot preparation, training completion, planting schedules, nursery supply and the terms communicated for that season.
