Market Access

The commercial pathway that connects farmer effort to income — from quality dried beans to credible exporters.

The Buying Model

How the buying model works

Farmers harvest and handle cocoa using Bulimi's quality guidance. Bulimi checks eligible dried beans, records purchases, aggregates volumes and markets the cocoa to exporters.

Organized supply can reduce the distance between farmer and buyer, improve quality consistency and strengthen bargaining through volume and information.

What farmers can expect

Quality requirements explained before sale.

Transparent weighing and purchase records.

Prices communicated clearly at the time of purchase.

Prompt payment through approved and secure payment methods.

Feedback where beans do not meet the agreed quality standard.

Quality Standards

Basic cocoa quality requirements

Harvest Maturity

Harvest only mature pods and follow the advised post-harvest process.

Fermentation

Ferment according to Bulimi's current guidance where required.

Drying

Dry beans thoroughly on a clean, raised or approved surface.

Cleanliness

Keep beans free from mould, smoke, chemicals, stones and other foreign matter.

Storage

Store in clean, dry bags and protect from moisture before sale.

On pricing

No responsible buyer can guarantee the highest price at all times. Bulimi aims for competitive, transparent pricing and stronger market access through quality, organized volume and buyer relationships.

For Buyers

For exporters and cocoa buyers

Bulimi is developing a traceable supply base in new cocoa-growing areas. We welcome buyer relationships that support clear specifications, quality improvement, responsible sourcing, dependable offtake and transparent commercial terms.

Early buyer engagement can help align farmer training and post-harvest systems with market needs.
