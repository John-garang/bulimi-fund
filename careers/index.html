Field & Extension 5 positions

Extension Workers

Mobilize, register, train and support smallholder cocoa farmers, serving as the primary link between Bulimi and participating farming communities.

Kyenjojo District + frequent field travel Reports to Agronomist
View role details

Key responsibilities

  • Mobilize and sensitize farmers, community leaders and farmer groups about Bulimi services and products.
  • Identify, screen, register and onboard eligible smallholder farmers.
  • Support parish and community-level farmer groups.
  • Conduct farmer training sessions and practical field demonstrations.
  • Guide farmers on land preparation, cocoa planting, shade management, soil fertility, pruning and farm maintenance.
  • Conduct regular farm visits and monitor recommended agricultural practices.
  • Identify and report pests, diseases and other production challenges.
  • Maintain farmer profiles, attendance records, farm data and field activity reports.
  • Support seedling and agricultural-input distribution and monitor seedling survival.
  • Promote agroforestry, climate-smart agriculture, traceability and responsible farming practices.

Minimum qualifications

  • Diploma or bachelor's degree in Agriculture, Agribusiness, Social Works, Business Administration or a related field.
  • At least one year of relevant experience in farmer mobilization, agricultural extension, community development or agribusiness field operations.
  • Experience working with smallholder farmers and farmer groups is highly desirable.
  • Practical cocoa-production knowledge is an added advantage.
  • Ability and willingness to travel extensively in rural communities.
  • Smartphone, Microsoft Office and digital data-collection skills.
  • Motorcycle riding ability and a valid riding permit are an added advantage.

Required competencies

  • Strong communication, facilitation and community-mobilization skills.
  • Ability to communicate in local languages used in target communities.
  • Good recordkeeping and report-writing skills.
  • Integrity, patience, adaptability and respect for farmers.
  • Ability to work independently and within a field-based team.

Agronomist

Provide technical leadership for Bulimi's cocoa production and farmer-support programmes, including farm establishment, soil management, agroforestry and sustainable agriculture.

Kyenjojo District + regular field travel Reports to Chief Operations Officer
View role details

Key responsibilities

  • Develop technical guidelines for cocoa farm establishment and management.
  • Design farmer-training materials, seasonal plans and practical demonstrations.
  • Train and supervise field teams delivering agricultural extension services.

  • Cocoa, perennial-crop or agroforestry experience is highly desirable.
  • Knowledge of organic agriculture, soil fertility and integrated pest management.
  • Experience supervising field teams and delivering farmer training.
  • Relevant professional-body membership is an added advantage.

Required competencies

  • Strong technical, analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Ability to translate technical knowledge into practical farmer guidance.
  • Leadership, coaching and team-supervision skills.
  • Strong planning, reporting and data-analysis ability.

Market Access 1 position

Market Access Officer

Coordinate cocoa purchasing, collection, transportation, fermentation, storage and quality control while supporting transparent farmer pricing and strong post-harvest practices.

Kyenjojo District + frequent community travel Reports to Chief Operations Officer
View role details

Core areas of responsibility

  • Cocoa purchasing: coordinate purchases, verify source, quantity and quality, and maintain complete purchase records.
  • Market research and pricing: monitor cocoa prices and market trends and recommend fair, competitive purchasing prices.
  • Collection and transportation: plan routes, schedules and logistics for moving cocoa safely from farms and collection points.
  • Fermentation and post-harvest: supervise fermentation, drying and hygienic handling of wet and dry cocoa.
  • Quality assurance: inspect cocoa, manage quality tests, grading, traceability and corrective actions.
  • Farmer technical support: train farmers on harvesting, fermentation, drying, moisture management, sorting and storage.
  • Stores and inventory: supervise receipt, weighing, grading, bagging, labelling, storage and stock reconciliation.
  • Team supervision: coordinate staff involved in cocoa collection, processing, storage and transportation.
  • Reporting and compliance: maintain purchasing, batch, payment, quality and inventory records and support audits and controls.

Minimum qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree or diploma in Agribusiness, Agriculture, Agricultural Economics, Procurement and Logistics, Supply Chain Management, Food Science, Post-Harvest Technology, Business Administration or a related field.
  • At least three years of relevant experience in commodity buying, cocoa procurement, aggregation, post-harvest management, warehouse operations or quality assurance.
  • Practical cocoa harvesting, fermentation, drying, grading, storage and quality-control knowledge is highly desirable.
  • Experience working with smallholder farmers and coordinating rural commodity transport.
  • Knowledge of commodity pricing, market research and purchasing procedures.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Excel and digital inventory or procurement systems.
  • Ability and willingness to travel regularly to farms and collection points.

Required competencies

  • Strong cocoa post-harvest and quality knowledge.
  • Purchasing, negotiation and supplier-relationship skills.
  • Market-analysis and pricing ability.
  • Strong logistics and coordination skills.
  • Integrity, transparency and accountability.
  • Inventory control and accurate recordkeeping.
  • Farmer-training and interpersonal skills.
  • Staff supervision and multitasking ability.
  • Strong numerical, analytical and reporting skills.
  • Commitment to fair farmer treatment and responsible sourcing.

Key performance areas

  • Cocoa purchasing volumes and quality performance.
  • Timeliness and efficiency of collection and transportation.
  • Accuracy of weighing, purchasing, batch and inventory records.
  • Reduction of post-harvest losses and rejected cocoa.
  • Fermentation, drying and stock-control performance.
  • Farmer satisfaction with purchasing, pricing and payment processes.

Nursery Operations 1 position

Nursery Bed Operations Officer

Manage Bulimi's cocoa nursery beds and ensure the timely production and distribution of healthy, high-quality cocoa seedlings and approved planting materials.

Kyenjojo District Reports to Agronomist
View role details

Key responsibilities

  • Plan and supervise establishment, maintenance and expansion of cocoa nursery beds.
  • Prepare nursery production plans based on farmer onboarding and planting targets.
  • Coordinate seed sourcing, potting, planting, watering and seedling maintenance.
  • Supervise nursery workers and allocate daily assignments.
  • Monitor germination, plant health, seedling survival, pests and diseases.
  • Maintain irrigation, drainage, shading, sanitation and nursery security.
  • Grade seedlings and distribute only healthy, mature and approved planting material.
  • Maintain production, stock, loss and seedling-distribution records.
  • Coordinate hardening, loading, transportation and farmer distribution.
  • Support propagation research, trials and nursery-management improvements.

Minimum qualifications

  • Diploma or bachelor's degree in Agriculture, Horticulture, Crop Science, Forestry, Nursery Management or a related field.
  • At least two years of practical nursery-management, seedling-production or crop-propagation experience.
  • Experience with cocoa, coffee, forestry or other perennial-crop seedlings is highly desirable.
  • Knowledge of plant nutrition, irrigation, pest and disease control and nursery hygiene.
  • Experience supervising casual or seasonal workers.

Required competencies

  • Strong practical nursery-management and supervisory skills.
  • Good planning and production-tracking ability.
  • Attention to plant health and quality standards.
  • Strong recordkeeping and reporting skills.
  • Ability to work effectively in an outdoor production environment.
