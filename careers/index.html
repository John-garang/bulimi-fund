Growth Opportunity

Inclusive Culture

Field-Centred Work

Collaborative Team

Mission-Driven Leadership

Future Roles

Areas we'll be hiring in

As we grow, we'll be building teams across these key areas. If your background fits, we'd love to hear from you.

1

Field & Extension

Extension officers, agronomists and field coordinators working directly with farmers on training, garden establishment and farm support.

2

Operations & Logistics

Roles managing nursery operations, input supply chains, aggregation logistics and quality control across our farmer network.

3

Finance & Administration

Finance officers, accountants and administrative staff supporting organisational compliance, reporting and financial management.

4

Monitoring & Evaluation

M&E officers and data analysts tracking farmer outcomes, programme performance and impact reporting.

5

Partnerships & Communications

Roles focused on donor relations, corporate partnerships, stakeholder engagement and external communications.

6

Market Access & Trade

Buyer relations, cocoa aggregation, quality grading and export coordination to connect farmers to reliable markets.

What We Look For

Our hiring principles

We hire people who are committed to farmer dignity, honest in their work, and motivated by practical outcomes over appearances. We value field experience, community understanding and a willingness to learn alongside the farmers we serve.

Equal Opportunity

Bulimi is an equal opportunity employer. We actively encourage applications from women, young people and candidates from rural and farming communities.

Safeguarding Commitment

All roles are subject to our safeguarding policy. Successful candidates will undergo reference checks and, where applicable, background screening. See our Safeguarding Policy.

Local Hiring Priority

We prioritise hiring from the communities and districts where we operate, particularly for field-based roles.
