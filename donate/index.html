Support Bulimi

Cocoa requires patient establishment. Your support helps farming families build a long-term livelihood asset during the critical years before the crop provides meaningful income.

Why Support Is Needed

The establishment gap

Farmers invest land, labour and care well before the crop provides meaningful income. Bulimi must prepare nursery beds, train farmers, help establish gardens and sustain extension through this critical period. Donations help close that gap and protect the quality of the investment.

Your support can enable

Farmer registration, plot mapping and community orientation.

Training in land preparation, planting and cocoa farm management.

Nursery operations and the production of high-quality seedlings.

Field extension, survival checks, gap filling and farm records.

Last-mile access to subsidised organic fertilizers and organic pesticides.

Farmer-group organization, quality systems and market preparation.

Traceability, safeguarding, monitoring and transparent reporting.

Ways to give

One-Time Gift

Support the immediate requirements of farmer establishment.

Monthly Support

Help sustain field services through the multi-year establishment period.

Corporate Partnership

Fund a defined workstream, geography or implementation milestone.

In-Kind Support

Provide approved equipment, inputs, transport, technical assistance or technology.

Our accountability commitment

Bulimi records restricted and unrestricted support, monitors funded activities and communicates progress against the purpose of each contribution. Donor information is handled responsibly and is not sold. Formal receipts and reports are provided according to the giving channel and agreement.

Bulimi is building farmer capability, productive cocoa gardens and dependable market access in Uganda.
