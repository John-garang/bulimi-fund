Support Bulimi

Cocoa requires patient establishment. Your support helps farming families build a long term livelihood asset during the critical years before the crop provides meaningful income.

Why Support Is Needed

The establishment gap

Farmers invest land, labour and care well before the crop provides meaningful income. Bulimi must prepare nursery beds, train farmers, help establish gardens and sustain extension through this critical period.

Donations help close that gap and protect the quality of the investment made in farming families and their cocoa gardens.

Your contribution

Your support can enable

Farmer registration, plot mapping and community orientation.
Training in land preparation, planting and cocoa farm management.
Nursery operations and the production of high quality seedlings.
Field extension, survival checks, gap filling and farm records.
Last mile access to subsidised organic fertilizers and organic pesticides.
Farmer group organization, quality systems and market preparation.
Traceability, monitoring and transparent reporting.
